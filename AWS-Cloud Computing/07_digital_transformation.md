## 🔥 What is Digital Transformation?

Digital transformation is the process of a company **abandoning old technology for new technology**, often motivated by the belief that the organization's future survival depends on it.

In the context of AWS, this is frequently visualized as jumping from a **"burning platform"** — such as an outdated on-premise data center — to the cloud.

### 🧰 AWS Tools for the Transition

| Tool | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF)** | A white paper organizing migration guidance into six focus areas |
| **Cloud Adoption Readiness Tool** | Survey-based tool producing assessment reports, heat maps, and radar charts to convince stakeholders |
| **Role Evolution** | Shifts IT staff from "heavy lifting" tasks (racking servers) to revenue-generating, specialized cloud roles |

### 🧭 CAF's Six Focus Areas

```
Business | People | Governance | Platform | Security | Operations
```

> 💡 **Tip:** The CAF isn't a technical checklist — it's a strategic framework covering people and process just as much as technology.

## 🌊 Innovation Waves (Kondratiev Waves)

Innovation waves, also known as **Kondratiev waves** or **K-waves**, are hypothesized economic cycles closely tied to technology life cycles.

### 📈 Key Characteristics

| Characteristic | Description |
|---|---|
| **Societal Change** | Each wave irreversibly changes society on a global scale |
| **Economic Pattern** | Follows a cycle: recession → depression → improvement |
| **Historical Examples** | Steam engine, railways, electric engineering, automobiles, information technology |
| **Latest Wave** | Cloud technology — potentially alongside Web3 and AI/Machine Learning |

> ⚠️ **Gotcha:** This is *why* organizations feel they're on a "burning platform" — waves are considered irreversible, so staying on the old wave isn't a neutral choice, it's a risk.

## ✅ Digital Transformation Checklist

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1️⃣ Strategize | Use the **CAF** across its six focus areas |
| 2️⃣ Assess Readiness | Run the **Cloud Adoption Readiness Tool** for a report, heat map, and radar chart |
| 3️⃣ Identify Burning Platform | Determine which old tech threatens survival and must be abandoned |
| 4️⃣ Shift IT Roles | Move staff from undifferentiated heavy lifting → revenue-generating / specialized roles |
| 5️⃣ Calculate TCO | Use **AWS Pricing Calculator** or **Migration Evaluator** to compare on-prem vs. cloud costs |

## ⚡ Evolution of Computing Power

Computing power is defined by the **throughput** at which a computer completes tasks. It has evolved in three stages — each stage *adds* a new layer of specialized power rather than replacing the old one.

| Stage | Hardware Example | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| 🖥️ **General Computing (CPU)** | Intel Xeon | Standard model used in AWS data centers for regular web workloads |
| 🎮 **Specialized Computing (GPU/Tensor)** | AWS Inferentia | Optimized for parallel operations — up to **50x faster** than CPUs; competes with Google TPUs for AI/ML |
| ⚛️ **Quantum Computing** | Rigetti 16Q Aspen-4 | Theoretically up to **100 million times faster**; accessible via **Amazon Braket**; applications currently limited |

> 💡 **Tip:** Don't think of this as a replacement ladder — a modern AWS workload might use CPU, GPU, *and* quantum resources side by side for different tasks.
